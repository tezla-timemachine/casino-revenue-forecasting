# Review of the 10 flagged revisions

Each figure below was flagged because the same month reads differently in the report a year later by more than 1%.

**Question:** is each change a real revision by the Gaming Control Board, or a mistake in our reading?

**How we tested it**

1. **Both readers agree**, so the extraction isn't the cause.
2. **Every county and statewide total adds up in both reports**, so each report is internally consistent.
3. **The monthly change matches the fiscal-year-to-date change.** Each report also shows the year-to-date figure (July to that month). If the monthly figure was revised, the year-to-date figure should move by the same amount. That's the strongest test here, because the two columns come from different parts of the report.

| Month (first report) | Area | Monthly change | Year-to-date change | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dec 2022 | Sparks | +$777,926 | +$826,443 | Real revision. Year-to-date moved too, plus earlier months in the same fiscal year |
| Jun 2023 | North Lake Tahoe | −$152,574 | −$152,610 | Real revision. Both columns agree |
| Aug 2023 | North Lake Tahoe | −$445,741 | −$435,476 | Real revision. Both columns move together, with other months in the year also revised |
| Sep 2023 | Other | +$203,388 | +$203,388 | Real revision. Both columns agree exactly |
| Jan 2024 | Other | +$188,485 | +$186,305 | Real revision. Both columns move together |
| May 2024 | North Lake Tahoe | +$58,820 | +$58,821 | Real revision. Both columns agree exactly |
| Oct 2024 | North Lake Tahoe | −$134,231 | −$134,232 | Real revision. Both columns agree exactly |
| Oct 2024 | Balance of Washoe County | −$104,387 | −$104,387 | Real revision. Both columns agree exactly |
| Dec 2024 | Other | −$200,641 | −$200,662 | Real revision. Both columns agree |
| May 2025 | Elko County, Balance | −$127,953 | −$139,896 | Real revision. Other months in the year also revised |

**Conclusion:** all 10 are genuine revisions in the Gaming Control Board's own figures, not errors in our reading. Under decision D9 we use the newest figure for each month, so these 10 months will use the revised values.

**One pattern to note:** North Lake Tahoe was revised by almost exactly the same proportion in June 2023 (−6.99%) and October 2024 (−6.99%). That's worth a question to the Board, because it could be a reclassification rather than a correction. It doesn't change how we handle the data.

**What goes in the dataset:** the newest figure for each month, plus a "revised" flag and the report it came from, so anyone can trace the change.
